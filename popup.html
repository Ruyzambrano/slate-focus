Slate Focus

Click on any article, then move your mouse down the page — everything dims except a band around your cursor. Click the button again to undo.

Focus Mode can't run on this page — browser pages like this one are off-limits to every extension. Try it on a regular website instead.

Want a keyboard shortcut for Focus Mode? Set one at chrome://extensions/shortcuts (search for Slate Focus).
